One short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance: you, alone at a
supermarket self-checkout, while the machine accuses your milk, your bread,
and finally your own hands of being "unexpected items".

> You scan the milk. 'Unexpected item in the bagging area.' You scan the
> bread. 'Unexpected item.' You lift the bag — it flags your own hands. The
> screen blinks: 'Please wait for assistance.' The assistant is on break.
> You are the assistance now.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Writes the single rant line (244 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.
